5  As-Is vs To-Be Comparison

DimensionAs-Is (today)To-Be (TK platform)
Process steps~9 manual steps across 5 actors, two divergent intake routes.~8 steps in one flow; single intake model for store & online; exceptions handled by TK-Flow.
Handoffs5–6 manual handoffs (paper→LINE→HQ→Finance→SAP), each a point of loss.0 manual handoffs; systems integrate via TK-Gateway / TK-Queue / TK-Connect.
Lead time~14 days avg (10–21), gated by the Friday SAP batch.≤ 3 business days (p95) — continuous, reconciled posting (BR-01).
Manual touchesRe-key at intake, re-key in Excel master, re-key in SAP — 3+ per return.Captured once; no re-keying; auto-posted to SAP.
Systems touchedPaper, Excel, LINE (consumer), email, SAP (manual) — 5 disconnected tools.One platform + TK-Connect to SAP/POS/payment + TK-Notify channels.
Approval & auditInformal LINE approval; no approver identity or immutable record.Structured TK-Flow DMN; immutable audit of every approval, refund, status & config change.
Money controlsManual SAP entry; no idempotency; no reconciliation; duplicate risk.Idempotency key per refund; daily reconciliation vs SAP; mismatch alerts (BRL-014, TR-REF-04).
Customer visibilityNone — customer chases; #1 social complaint (~2,300/qtr).Proactive notifications each step + tracking page; complaint baseline to reduce ≥50% (BR-02).
Returned goodsPiles behind store; no decision; value lost.Mandatory disposition per item (RESTOCK / RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER / WRITE_OFF) (BR-04).
FraudInvisible — no flags.Configurable threshold flags serial returners to CS (BR-05).
ReportingAd-hoc Excel; no executive view.Daily-refresh exec dashboard via TK-DataLake; Excel/PDF export; daily Finance refund report (BR-07).

Net effect. The To-Be removes every manual handoff and re-key, cuts the lead time from ~14 days to ≤3 business days, gives Finance an auditable and reconciled money trail, and turns returned goods and fraud from blind spots into governed flows — all traced to BR-01…BR-08 (§13).
