Status & relationship to requirements

This is a v1.0 draft for customer review. It designs the SiamMart returns solution on the TK platform and traces every element back to the IDs in the requirements package TK-BA-SMR-REQ-01 (TR-, NFR-, INT-, BRL-, BR-). Open issues are carried forward unchanged as OI-; they are not silently resolved here. Where the requirements raised a conflict, this architecture implements a safe, configurable default and flags the decision gate.

Reading note. Diagrams use the skill D-numbering D1–D8 (§6–§11 and §2/§4). Every diagram is inline SVG and carries the in-SVG "TK CONFIDENTIAL" label. Wide diagrams are on landscape pages. Fonts in diagrams are ≥ 9pt.
