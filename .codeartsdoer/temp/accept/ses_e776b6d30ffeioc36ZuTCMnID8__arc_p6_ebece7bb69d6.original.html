6  System Context — C4 Level 1 (D3)

The SiamMart Returns Platform is a single TK-managed SaaS system. It serves SiamMart staff and customers and exchanges data with external systems only through TK-Connect (SAP/POS/payment), TK-Notify (SMS/LINE/email) or TK-Auth (Active Directory). No actor or external system talks to the platform database directly.
