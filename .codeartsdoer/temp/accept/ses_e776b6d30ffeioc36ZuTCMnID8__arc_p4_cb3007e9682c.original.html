4  To-Be Process

The To-Be collapses the five As-Is tools into one platform (one system of record) and turns rule-based decisions into a configurable rules engine (TK-Flow DMN), keeping humans only for exceptions and approvals that genuinely need judgement. Every manual handoff and re-key is removed; the Friday SAP batch becomes a continuous, idempotent, reconciled posting.
