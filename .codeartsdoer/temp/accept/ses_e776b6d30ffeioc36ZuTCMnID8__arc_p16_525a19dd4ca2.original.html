12.2  Consequences flagged to the customer

  • SAP access: no direct RFC — only TK-Connect (ADR-01). Credentials offered for direct use will be declined.
  • Marketplace: Shopee/Lazada returns are Phase 2 (ADR-07); pulling them into Phase 1 needs a CR + budget.
  • SMS cost: recurring per-message fees scale with 25k returns and the 4-5x peak (ADR-05) — budget explicitly.
  • Photo retention: item photos retained per NFR-DATA-01/AS-03 (7 yrs, pending OI-06); encrypted, access-logged, auto-purged at retention end.
  • Auto-approval: Finance/Audit written sign-off required to enable (ADR-08 / OI-01).
  • POS vendor & AD availability (OI-08, OI-07) gate the exact integration build.
