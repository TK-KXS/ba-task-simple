7.3  TK Platform Tools Used (and why)

Every internal component is a standard TK platform service; no bespoke infrastructure and no third-party product sits inside the trust boundary. The only non-TK components are the external systems the customer already owns (SAP, POS, payment, messaging, AD), each with an ADR in section 12.
