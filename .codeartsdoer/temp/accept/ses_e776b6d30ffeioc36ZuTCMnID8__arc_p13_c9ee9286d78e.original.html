10.3  PDPA controls implemented

  • Lawful basis & consent: consent captured at first data entry; purpose limitation recorded (ROPA maintained by TK as processor).
  • Data-subject rights: access and erasure request workflow; erasure honoured except where legal/audit retention applies (7 yrs) — with justification logged.
  • Masking: PII and financial fields masked in logs, dashboards and non-production environments; production access least-privilege via TK-Auth.
  • Retention + deletion job: scheduled job purges personal data and photos at retention end (default 7 yrs, subject to OI-06); deletes storage objects and database rows, leaving only anonymised audit references.
  • Breach runbook and Data Processing Agreement between SiamMart (controller) and TK (processor).
PDPA caveat (K.Arthit). Retention period is assumption AS-03 (7 years) pending audit/OI-06 and OI-12 (migration cut-off). The retention job is configurable and must be set to the customer-confirmed value before go-live; photo retention (7 yrs) is one of the customer's explicit concerns.
